適用範圍 Scope

Housing Radar n8n 為個人自用的自動化工具，唯一使用者為本工具的擁有者本人，不對外提供服務。

Housing Radar n8n is a personal automation tool used solely by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

存取的資料 Data accessed

本工具僅存取擁有者本人的 Google 帳戶資料：讀取 Gmail 中的 Google 快訊信件、寄送通知信給本人、將快訊信件移至垃圾桶，以及讀寫本人擁有的 Google 試算表。

The tool only accesses the owner's own Google account: reading Google Alerts emails in Gmail, sending notification emails to the owner, moving processed alert emails to trash, and reading/writing Google Sheets owned by the owner.

資料使用與分享 Use and sharing

資料僅用於擁有者本人的房地產研究，不會出售、分享或提供給任何第三方。新聞標題與摘要會傳送至 AI 服務（Groq）進行整理，僅包含公開新聞內容。

Data is used only for the owner's own real-estate research and is never sold or shared with third parties. Public news titles and snippets are sent to an AI service (Groq) for summarization.

資料儲存 Storage

處理結果儲存在擁有者本人的 Google 試算表中。擁有者可隨時於 Google 帳戶設定中撤銷本工具的存取權限。

Results are stored in the owner's own Google Sheets. Access can be revoked at any time in Google Account settings.
