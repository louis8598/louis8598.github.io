Housing Radar n8n

這是個人使用的研究自動化工具，透過 n8n 讀取本人 Gmail 中的 Google 快訊、整理房地產新聞，並寫入本人擁有的 Google 試算表，僅供本人研究使用。

This is a personal research automation tool. It reads Google Alerts emails from the owner's own Gmail account via n8n, organizes real-estate news, and writes results to Google Sheets owned by the same person. It is not offered to any other users.
